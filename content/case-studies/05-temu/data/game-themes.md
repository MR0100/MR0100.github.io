# Temu App Store reviews: the game layer, coded

**Sample.** `appstore-reviews-10-storefronts.json`: 450 unique reviews from Apple's public review feed for Temu (id 1641486558), pulled 10 Oct 2026. Most recent and most helpful, from GB (150), CA (100), NZ (100), IN (50) and IE (50). The US, AU, SG, AE and ZA feeds returned nothing. Dates: 21 Sep 2022 – 8 Oct 2026. Ratings: 215 five-star, 47 four, 19 three, 19 two, 150 one. The "most helpful" sort leans older and longer, so this is not a random sample.

**Method.** A keyword pass found 111 reviews that mention the game layer (games, spin, wheel, free gifts, Fishland/Farmland, coins, points, invites, referrals, prizes). I read every match in context and removed false positives. A review can count in more than one theme. Counts are by one coder (Mitul), so treat them as indicative.

**A reward people praise.** 13 reviews (of all 450) mention the automatic credit for a late delivery (£4/$5) or the price-drop adjustment, and 11 of them praise it (10 at 5★). Ids: 435072, 831648, 569527, 261026, 979710, 698714, 196977, 979617, 520414, 973093, 382245, 494332, 745677. One (196977) says the credit offer appeared and then disappeared.

**What this says.** People don't hate the game. Several 5★ reviewers enjoy it and keep playing. The anger is about the contract: progress that slows without saying so, invites that only count for brand-new users, and "free" that turns into a minimum spend after the user has committed. The most-praised reward is the simplest one: a clear rule that pays out automatically.
