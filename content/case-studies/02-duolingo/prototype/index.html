Earn it back: a way home after a broken streak

When a long streak breaks, it resets to zero and the way back costs gems. In June 2026, 15.4 million learners took a one-month offer to revive a lost streak, and they kept learning better than a typical returning group. Then the offer ended. This makes the way back permanent.

Fairness and trust
What to take from this

Three lessons for any app

  1. Treat a break as a pause, not a reset. Keep lifetime effort visible.
  2. Remove friction when people try to come back. That is when they are most motivated and have the least to spend.
  3. Earn it back through practice rather than a purchase, and mark it honestly, so the number still means learning.
What the user test will decide

Does a safety net make people practise less on busy days? If 3 of 5 testers say yes, or say a restored streak “doesn’t count”, the idea needs rethinking.
