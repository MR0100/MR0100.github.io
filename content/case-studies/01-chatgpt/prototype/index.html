Visible allowance: a softer landing at the usage limit

Free users find the photo and image tools. What makes them leave is a limit they couldn’t see, arriving in the middle of a task. In 147 negative App Store reviews, 54 were about usage limits and only 1 was about finding a feature.

Independent study by Mitul Vaghasiya, not affiliated with ChatGPT. Every screen below is my re-creation in ChatGPT’s visual style, based on its App Store screenshots (10 Oct 2026) and the public images listed in references/. They are not screenshots of the real app.

Trust and predictability
What to take from this

Three lessons for any app

  1. Put the meter where the decision happens, not in a settings page.
  2. Warn before the limit, not after. A surprise at the moment of highest intent is what people remember.
  3. Give more than one way out. Upgrading works better as one choice among several than as the only exit.
What the user test will decide

Does seeing the meter make people ration their use? If 3 of 5 testers hold back because they can see the count, the idea needs rethinking.
