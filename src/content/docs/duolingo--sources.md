## States with no public reference found

- **Streak-lost screen (variant A break screen, a0).** No image from a reputable publisher or Duolingo shows the current screen after a streak is lost. Android Authority's articles (energy, June 2026 revival) were blocked to automated download. Re-created from the real streak screen (App Store 06.jpg: big number, "day streak", week card) in grey.
- **Streak Repair offer and its price (a0, a1).** No verified current price or time window. Third-party guides disagree (tiered gem prices, a 48-hour window, free monthly repair for subscribers). The 350-gem price and the "Not enough gems" sheet remain placeholders.
- **Out-of-energy screen and gem shop.** Not shown in the prototype; no reputable image found.
- **Freeze price (b7).** The 100-gem price is a placeholder.
