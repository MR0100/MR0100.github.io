Duolingo makes you sign up last.

Guess which one people actually finish.

The usual flow:
Create account → verify email → set password → finally see the app.
Every step is a reason to leave.

Duolingo's flow:
1️⃣ "What do you want to learn?"
2️⃣ Pick a daily goal
3️⃣ Do a real lesson in under a minute
4️⃣ Then: "Create a profile to save your progress"

Why it works:
- You feel progress before you're asked for anything
- By the time it asks, you have something to lose: your progress and your streak
- "Save your progress" feels like help, not a gate

What it costs to build: guest accounts, local progress, and merging guest data into a real account later.

Not every app can do this. Banking and health apps often need identity first. But most apps ask for sign-up much earlier than they need to.

Could your app let people try it before signing up? What's stopping you?
