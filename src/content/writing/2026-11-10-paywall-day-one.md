That's from RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 report, which compares two ways to charge:

Hard paywall: you pay, or start a trial, before you can use the app.
Freemium: you use it for free and upgrade later.

The money gap is huge (medians):
- 10.7% vs 2.1% become paying customers by day 35
- $3.09 vs $0.38 revenue per install by day 60

Now the twist. After one year, 27% of yearly subscribers are still paying with a hard paywall. With freemium, it's 28%.

So charging early brings in more money upfront. It doesn't make people stay.

My take: the paywall decides who starts paying. The product decides who stays.

If people can see the value in the first minute, ask early. If the value takes a week to show up (a habit, a network, trust), let them in first.
