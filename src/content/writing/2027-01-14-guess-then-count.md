ChatGPT: I thought people couldn't find the features. They were hitting limits.
Revolut: I thought the app was cluttered. People were angry about money on hold with no timeline.
Duolingo: I thought streaks were too much pressure. People wanted theirs back.

What made the difference wasn't reading more reviews. It was how I read them:

1. Write the guess down first, in one sentence. Otherwise you'll find whatever you already believed.
2. Read the five-star reviews too. They tell you what to protect.
3. Count, with a review id next to every number. A table beats a quote.
4. Quote exactly, with rating, country and date.
5. Name what the sample can't tell you.

That turns "I feel users hate X" into "54 of 147 negative reviews are about X, and here they are."

What did your reviews tell you that your team didn't believe?
