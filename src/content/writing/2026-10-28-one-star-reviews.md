Most teams read them, feel bad, and do nothing.

Interviews tell you what people say.
Reviews tell you what made them angry enough to write, unprompted, at the worst moment.

Here's how I turn hundreds of reviews into a prioritised list with AI, in an afternoon:

1️⃣ Collect: export reviews for your app and 2–3 competitors
2️⃣ Clean: remove duplicates and one-word reviews
3️⃣ Cluster: ask the AI for themes, with a count and 3 real quotes each
4️⃣ Check: read the quotes yourself. AI groups; you judge.
5️⃣ Rank: impact × effort
6️⃣ Validate: test the top theme with 5 real users before building

The prompt I start with:
"Group these reviews into themes. For each, give a name, the number of reviews and 3 direct quotes. Don't invent themes that aren't in the data."

To be fair to interviews: reviews tell you what's wrong, but interviews tell you why. Use reviews to know which questions to ask.
