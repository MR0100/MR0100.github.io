Not on day 3, when the card gets charged. On the day people sign up.

That's from RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026.

So the trial isn't decided by the trial. It's decided in the first session. People start a trial, look around, and if they don't see the value right then, they cancel before they forget.

Before blaming the trial length, I'd check three things:
- Does the first session end with a real result? A finished lesson, a first edit, a saved plan.
- Is the paywall the first thing people see, before they've done anything?
- Do your reminders say what people get, or only when they'll pay?

Length matters too. In the same report, trials of 17–32 days convert at a 42.5% median, against 25.5% for trials under 4 days.

Onboarding is your real paywall.

What's the first thing a new user achieves in your app?
