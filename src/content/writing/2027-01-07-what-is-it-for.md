I replaced the flashing spin wheel with a simple offer card and a clear rules sheet. It fixed every complaint in the reviews.

And it removed the reason millions of people open the app.

The games do real work:
- a daily habit
- cheap growth through invites
- browsing that turns into buying

In 450 App Store reviews, players said they enjoy the games. What they resented was the rules: 17 said progress crawls near the end ("99.99%"), 14 said "free" turned into a minimum spend.

So the final design kept the wheel, the confetti and the countdowns, and changed only the rules: shown before you play, worth the same at every step, and true.

Before any redesign I now write three lists:
1. The job: what it does for the business.
2. Protect: what people enjoy.
3. Fix: what exactly they resent.

Which feature would you have removed, and what would it have broken?
