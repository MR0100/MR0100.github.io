That's not my cynicism. Duolingo's own team has written about it.

In a 2022 post, they explain that longer streaks tap into loss aversion: we hate losing something more than we enjoy gaining it.

And they designed around it:
- Every day you keep the streak, there's more to lose
- A Streak Freeze lets you miss a day without losing it
- Letting people hold up to two Freezes increased daily active learners by 0.38%

They also report that learners who reach a 7-day streak are 3.6 times more likely to finish their course.

Is that manipulation? I don't think so, as long as two things are true:
1. The habit is worth having. Daily language practice is.
2. There's a way out. The Freeze makes a missed day forgivable, not a punishment.

When the product isn't worth the habit, the same streak becomes a dark pattern: people come back out of fear, not value.

Would your app's streak pass that test?
