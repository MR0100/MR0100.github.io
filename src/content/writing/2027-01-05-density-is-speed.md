I read 955 public reviews of diamond-trading apps for a case study. The complaints after redesigns were never about colours.

They were about speed:
- "Only 3 stones we can see."
- Seller initials instead of full names.
- "Have to do it individually."

Design advice is written for beginners: more space, one thing per screen, hide the detail.
For experts, that's slower. Psychologists call it the expertise reversal effect.

What I do on expert screens now:
1. Count the items per screen today. Keep that number.
2. Add beside, not on top: a thumbnail, never a hero image.
3. Label instead of hiding.
4. Show what experts check every time.
5. Keep controls where they are.

Experts don't resist change. They resist losing speed.
