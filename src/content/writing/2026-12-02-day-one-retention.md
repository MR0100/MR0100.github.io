Adjust's 2026 benchmark of thousands of apps: games keep 27% of new users the next day, and shopping and finance apps only about 12%. By day 30, it's 5% for games, 3% for shopping and 2% for finance.

That drop isn't random. Most of it happens because the first session never gave people anything.

4 first-session fixes I'd try before any "we miss you" email:
1. Get people to one real result in under a minute.
2. Ask for sign-up, permissions and notifications only when they're needed.
3. Cut every screen that's about the app (tours, feature lists) instead of about them.
4. End the first session with a reason to come back: a saved draft, a plan for tomorrow, a reminder they chose.

Retention isn't fixed with emails. It's fixed in the first minute.
