I use AI to write code all the time. It has made me faster than ever.

But building was never the slow part of a failed app.

The slow part is the months after launch, when you find out nobody needed it.

A working prototype hides a lot:
- It proves you can build it. Not that anyone wants it.
- Friends say "looks great". Strangers don't come back.
- "I'd use this" costs nothing to say. Paying does.

So before I build something new, I have a few short conversations first, and ask:
1. When did you last have this problem?
2. What did you do about it?
3. What did that cost you, in time or money?

If nobody has a recent story, there's no product yet. Just a demo.

AI made building cheap. That makes knowing what to build the expensive part.

What did AI make faster for you, and what didn't it fix?
