After 7 years of turning designs into apps, these are the 5 things that cause most of the fights:

1. Safe areas. Notches, rounded corners and home bars eat your layout.
2. The keyboard. It covers half the screen. Where does "Continue" go?
3. Bigger text. Many users increase font size. Does your layout survive 130%?
4. Tap targets. ~44pt on iOS, 48dp on Android, even if the icon is tiny.
5. Platform habits. iOS and Android users expect different back, share and date-picker behaviour.

None of these show up in a static Figma frame. All of them show up in 1-star reviews.

And developers, we're not innocent: we should flag these before building, not after.

The best teams I've worked with check these 5 together, in the first design review.
