Designers won't love this one. Hear me out.

Animation isn't the problem. Decoration is.

Good animation answers one question: "What just happened?"
- Where did that item go? (It shrank into the cart.)
- Where am I now? (The screen slid in from the right, so back is left.)
- Did my tap work? (The button pressed down.)

Bad animation answers nothing:
- Logos that bounce on every launch
- 800 ms transitions that make a fast app feel slow
- Motion that ignores the "Reduce motion" setting

My rule as a developer:
If removing an animation makes the app confusing, keep it.
If removing it makes the app faster and nothing is lost, delete it.

Designers, where do you draw the line? Defend your favourite animation.
