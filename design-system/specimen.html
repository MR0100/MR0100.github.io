Design system·v1·Oct 2026

Calm pages, honest evidence.

The base layer copies Holstee's article system: warm paper, white reading card, Figtree Light headings, Inter body, a single teal accent and 10px corners. On top, our layer adds what case studies need: labels for evidence and hypotheses, severity dots, facts rows and framed screens.
